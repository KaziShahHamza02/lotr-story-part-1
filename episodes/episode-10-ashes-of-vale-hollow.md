# Episode 10: Ashes of Vale Hollow

**Status:** Complete  
**Word count:** 631 (story only)

## Summary

Before the company steps onto the frozen plain, a breathless rider from the southern valleys brings news of what became of Vale Hollow and Elder Tomren after the night of the beacon—and warns that another force is already climbing toward the pass.

## Story

Before Lira could give the order to march onto the ice, a lookout on the southern lip of the pass raised his stave.

"Rider!" he called. "Single horse, no pennant!"

Mara’s hand went straight to her scarf. Twenty wardens shifted along the broken shelter walls, arrows nocked and aimed down the switchback.

Out of the wind-blown snow climbed a shaggy bay pony, foam frozen along its chest. Its rider slumped forward over the saddle—not a soldier in dull armor, but a boy scarcely older than Fen, wearing a soot-streaked sheepskin vest and a miller’s cap pulled low over his ears.

"Hold," Mara cried, stepping past Lira’s stave. "I know him! That’s Bram, from Vale Hollow’s mill!"

The boy slid off the pony into the snow, his knees buckling. Mara and Calder caught him under the arms and dragged him behind the shelter wall, where Fen pressed a waterskin into his shaking hands.

"Mara?" Bram blinked through frost-crusted lashes, staring at her as if she were a ghost. "The beacon—you lit the Hearth?"

"We did," Mara said, kneeling beside him. "Bram, what happened after we left? Where is Elder Tomren?"

Bram swallowed hard, his teeth clicking against the wooden stopper. "He’s alive. The whole village is alive."

Fen let out a sound halfway between a laugh and a sob. Mara felt her shoulders drop an inch, as if a stone had been lifted from her chest.

"The eastern captain left twenty men to hold the square when he rode north after you," Bram said, rubbing feeling back into his fingers. "Tomren barred the storehouse doors with the village axe. We thought they’d burn us out by midnight."

"Then why didn't they?" Lira asked sharply.

"Because the western riders hit the square before the torches caught," Bram said.

Mara remembered the second horn from Episode 1—deep and heavy, answering from the western road just as she and Fen fled through the gate. "Who were they?"

"Border-men from the river towns," Bram answered. "They wore grey wool and carried iron-rimmed shields—no red pennants anywhere. Their marshal drove the eastern lancers out past the barley fields before dawn. The watchtower’s roof caved in when the white fire cooled, and the old well stopped ringing, but Vale Hollow still stands."

Calder frowned. "If the border-men saved your village, why did you ride half to death up this pass?"

Bram’s face tightened. He reached into his sheepskin vest and pulled out a folded square of rough parchment sealed with grey wax. "Because their marshal didn't stay in the square. He asked Tomren where you went, then took thirty riders north along the old road. Tomren sent me by the shepherd trails to warn you—he doesn't know whether those grey shields mean to help you, or take the needle for themselves."

Mara broke the wax seal. Inside, written in Tomren’s crooked, steady hand, were only six words:

*Keep moving. Trust no rider’s horn.*

Lira looked down the pass toward the valley mist, her jaw set. "Thirty horsemen on the lower road will reach the Stone Hearth by midday, and the pass before dark."

"Then we don't wait for dark," Mara said, folding Tomren’s note and tucking it beside the Keeper’s slate.

Bram struggled to stand, leaning against the stone wall. "I can’t ride that pony back down—not yet."

"You don't have to," Lira told him, gesturing to two of her wardens. "Stay with the rear guard if you can walk, or ride slow behind the line. Once we cross the plain, there’s nowhere left to hide on either side."

Mara looked back once toward the distant southern valleys, hidden now beneath grey cloud. Vale Hollow had survived the night, and Elder Tomren was still standing.

Now they only had to survive the riders coming up from the west.

## Continuity notes

- **Bram**, a boy from Vale Hollow’s mill, reaches the pass on a bay pony with a message from **Elder Tomren**.
- Vale Hollow survived the night of the beacon: the **western horn force**—thirty border-men in grey wool with iron-rimmed shields—drove the remaining eastern riders out of the village square.
- Vale Hollow’s watchtower roof collapsed when the white fire cooled, and the old well stopped ringing.
- Elder Tomren warns Mara in a brief note (*"Keep moving. Trust no rider’s horn."*) because the border-men’s marshal rode north after her, and his true motives toward the brass needle are unknown.
- Bram joins the rear of the company as they prepare to cross the frozen plain ahead of the approaching western riders.
